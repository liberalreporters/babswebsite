About Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

A professional profile based on biographical information supplied for this website.

Professional background and public profile

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS, is an accounting professional and corporate executive identified in the supplied profile as the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for Ibarapa East Constituency in the Oyo State House of Assembly election.

The supplied biography records that he was born in Eruwa on 20 August 1980. His education includes accounting studies at Federal Polytechnic, Ede; The Polytechnic, Ibadan; Crescent University; and the University of Lagos.

His stated professional background includes finance, accounting, internal control and executive management. The profile identifies him as Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd.

Professional experience

The supplied material records experience in accounting, finance management and business leadership, including a previous role with Portal Realities and a current leadership role at Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd. Exact job titles and dates should be verified against employment records.

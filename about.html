About BABS

Learning, professional growth and service-minded ambition.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS, is an accounting professional and corporate executive identified in the supplied profile as the All Progressives Congress (APC) aspirant for Ibarapa East Constituency in the Oyo State House of Assembly election.

Born in Eruwa on 20 August 1980, his educational journey includes studies at Apode High School, Federal Polytechnic, Ede, The Polytechnic, Ibadan, Crescent University and the University of Lagos. His professional background is described in the supplied material as spanning accounting, finance management, internal control and executive leadership.

The profile identifies him as Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd. It also records a previous role at Portal Realities between 2020 and 2024.

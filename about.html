About Babatunde

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

A Commitment to Representation, Community Development and Public Service

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is a Nigerian political figure, accountant, entrepreneur and public affairs advocate whose political journey is closely associated with Eruwa, Lanlate and the wider Ibarapa East Constituency of Oyo State. He is the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly, Ibarapa East Constituency, in the 2027 electoral cycle.

His stated approach to public life centres on accessibility, responsible leadership, inclusive development and constructive engagement with the people. It reflects the belief that effective representation takes more than political visibility. It calls for working knowledge of community needs, a willingness to listen, respect for local realities and the ability to turn public concerns into practical proposals within the framework of democratic governance.

Background

Rooted in Eruwa and Ibarapa East

Eruwa holds an important place in the history, culture and social life of Ibarapa. Its traditional institutions, community networks, commercial activity, schools and agricultural heritage have helped shape the identity of the wider region.

Growing up with ties to Eruwa and the Ibarapa environment gave Ogunlade an appreciation of the relationships, economic activities and community structures that shape everyday life in the area. Access to markets, roads, schools, healthcare and reliable public services strongly influences quality of life in rural and semi-urban communities, and understanding these realities is the starting point for meaningful representation.

Ibarapa East is not defined only by its challenges. It is home to hardworking farmers, traders, artisans, professionals, educators, business owners, students and young people whose efforts sustain local communities. Its traditional institutions and community associations preserve heritage, help resolve local concerns and strengthen social bonds. A development-oriented approach must recognise these strengths while finding practical ways to address the difficulties residents face.

“Vision to Lead. Capacity to Deliver.”

Education

A sustained commitment to learning

Ogunlade's academic training in accounting provides a foundation for understanding financial management, organisational administration, business operations and accountability in the use of resources.

  • Apode High School
  • National Diploma in Accounting, Federal Polytechnic, Ede (2002)
  • Higher National Diploma in Accounting, The Polytechnic, Ibadan (2006)
  • B.Sc. Accounting, Crescent University (2020)
  • M.Sc. Accounting, University of Lagos (2025)
Professional experience

A management perspective

Beyond the classroom, Ogunlade's private-sector experience has exposed him to the practical demands of organisational management, financial administration, business development and institutional coordination.

  • Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd
  • Deputy Managing Director and General Manager, Finance, Portal Realities (2020 to 2024)

Private-sector management and legislative service work within different institutional frameworks, but both call for planning, informed decision-making, clear communication and an understanding of the link between resources and results. In public representation, this perspective can inform discussion on budget priorities, economic development, public spending and legislative oversight, always within the constitutional responsibilities of the legislature.

Political journey

Participation, engagement and responsibility

Ogunlade has identified with the All Progressives Congress since 2003. His political journey reflects an interest in party organisation, stakeholder engagement and the ways individuals and groups take part in democratic life.

Political participation at constituency level involves more than elections. It includes engagement with ward structures, consultation with party members and conversations with community stakeholders. In a constituency where personal relationships and community networks matter, sustained engagement is especially relevant, and candidates must recognise that public expectations reach beyond party membership.

As the APC candidate for Ibarapa East, Ogunlade accepts the responsibilities of seeking elective office: presenting a clear programme, explaining policy intentions, engaging with competing perspectives and remaining answerable to the electorate. His stated approach emphasises building relationships across the constituency and encouraging constructive dialogue on the issues that affect residents.

Leadership philosophy

Representation that is accessible, responsive and realistic

At the centre of Ogunlade's platform is the principle that representation should be accessible, responsive and informed by the everyday experience of constituents. Through lawmaking, budget consideration, oversight, public engagement and advocacy, elected representatives can bring community concerns into formal decision-making.

Meaningful representation also requires an honest understanding of institutional roles. A member of the State House of Assembly can sponsor bills, take part in debates, scrutinise public spending, contribute to the budget process and advocate for the constituency. But many development projects depend on executive agencies, available funding and government procedures. Recognising this helps set realistic expectations and keeps accountability meaningful.

Under the theme “Vision to Lead. Capacity to Deliver.”, the platform stresses the link between leadership, competence and practical outcomes. Development is rarely achieved through isolated interventions. Education, agriculture, infrastructure, healthcare, enterprise and employment are connected, and a sound legislative agenda must weigh immediate concerns, long-term opportunities, the responsibilities of the state government and the resources available.

A public standard

Representation beyond party lines

The APC platform is the political structure through which Hon. Ogunlade seeks the mandate of Ibarapa East, but representation extends beyond party lines. An elected member serves the constituency as a whole, and the concerns of residents do not become irrelevant because they support another party or disagree on particular issues.

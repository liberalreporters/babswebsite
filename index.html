About

Leadership grounded in community realities.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is known for a public service approach shaped by listening, accountability, and practical engagement with the needs of local communities.

His platform reflects a commitment to public trust, grassroots connection, and action-oriented development that improves everyday life.

“Our people deserve a representative who listens, acts, and remains accountable to the communities he serves.”

— Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

The Ogunlade Blueprint

A practical plan for representation, opportunity and lasting progress.

FOCUS — Forward-thinking Opportunities for Community Unity and Sustainability — brings together proposed priorities for education, youth opportunity, community development and enterprise.

01

Education & human capital

Advocacy for better learning environments, student support, teacher development and practical skills.

02

Youth & opportunity

Stronger links to training, mentorship, enterprise programmes and youth participation in public life.

03

Community development

Advocacy on roads, water, electricity, primary healthcare and fair attention to every community.

04

Enterprise & sustainability

Support for farmers, traders, artisans and small businesses, with attention to long-term sustainability.

These are proposed priorities and stated commitments, not claims that the proposed outcomes have already been delivered.

Transparency starts with clear expectations

The policy areas on this website are proposals and advocacy priorities, not a record of completed government projects or guaranteed outcomes. Legislative action depends on due process, available resources, collaboration with executive agencies and public oversight. Progress should be communicated with evidence, dates and clear explanations.

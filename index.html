About

A representative committed to action, accountability, and community impact.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is a dedicated public servant and grassroots mobilizer with a strong commitment to the welfare of families, youth, women, workers, and vulnerable members of the community. He believes that effective representation must be visible, responsive, and rooted in the realities of the people.

His campaign is centered on transforming inclusion into action by delivering practical solutions in infrastructure, education, agriculture, healthcare access, youth empowerment, and transparent governance for Ibarapa East.

“Our people deserve a representative who will listen to them, stand with them, and ensure that nobody is left behind.”

— Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

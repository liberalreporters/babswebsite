The Ogunlade Blueprint

FOCUS: opportunities, community and sustainability.

The blueprint groups proposed areas of attention around people, local services and long-term community development. These are stated priorities for discussion—not claims that outcomes have already been delivered.

01

Education & human capital

Advocacy for improved learning environments, student support, teacher development and practical skills.

02

Youth & enterprise

Stronger pathways to training, mentorship, entrepreneurship and opportunities for young people.

03

Community infrastructure

Evidence-led advocacy on roads, water, electricity and the maintenance of public facilities.

04

Agriculture & markets

Attention to farmers, traders, artisans, local value chains and access to markets.

05

Healthcare access

Engagement with relevant authorities on primary care readiness, outreach and essential services.

06

Open representation

Clear communication, community consultation and public reporting on progress and challenges.

The priorities on this website describe proposed advocacy and stated intentions. They should not be interpreted as completed government projects, guaranteed outcomes or a record of legislative delivery.

Publication standards

  • Statements should carry a clear date, title and attribution.
  • Corrections should be logged transparently, with the original publication context preserved where practical.
  • Images should include captions, dates where known, and accurate alternative text.
  • Draft proposals must not be described as enacted policy, funded projects or completed achievements.

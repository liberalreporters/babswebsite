Proposed policy priorities

The Ogunlade public priorities

Stated areas of interest for representation, advocacy and legislative engagement in Ibarapa East.

Important: These are proposed priorities and statements of intent, not claims that any project, grant, service or policy outcome has already been delivered.
01

Education and human capital

  • Advocacy for improved learning environments and public-school support.
  • Exploration of scholarship and student-support opportunities.
  • Vocational, technical and digital skills development.
02

Youth and opportunity

  • Access to information about training and enterprise programmes.
  • Mentorship and skills opportunities.
  • Youth consultation and participation in public life.
03

Community development

  • Advocacy for roads, water and essential public services.
  • Engagement on primary healthcare and community needs.
  • Fair consideration of concerns across communities and wards.
04

Enterprise and sustainability

  • Advocacy for farmers, traders, artisans and small businesses.
  • Links to relevant training and enterprise-support programmes.
  • Responsible local development and environmental stewardship.
05

Representation and accountability

  • Accessible ways to raise constituency concerns.
  • Constructive legislative work and appropriate oversight.
  • Public reporting on activities, progress and limitations.
06

Community consultation

  • Engagement with residents and relevant stakeholders.
  • Recording and communicating recurring concerns.
  • Respectful dialogue across communities and backgrounds.

Role and limits of a state lawmaker

A member of the Oyo State House of Assembly can participate in lawmaking, representation, oversight and constituency advocacy within the powers and procedures of the House. Many services and projects also depend on executive agencies, budgets, appropriations, partnerships and other authorities. This page does not imply that a legislator can unilaterally guarantee every proposed outcome.

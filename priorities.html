Proposed public priorities

The Ogunlade Blueprint for Ibarapa East

A Practical Plan for Representation, Opportunity and Lasting Progress

Ibarapa East deserves a voice that is heard, a representative who is present, and a plan that turns promise into progress. This blueprint sets out how Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, APC candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly, intends to serve the people of Ibarapa East Constituency.

A message to the people

Representation rooted in the community

Our land is rich in farmland, heritage, enterprise and talent. Our people are hardworking, proud and ready. Ibarapa East needs representation that brings these strengths into the rooms where decisions are made and keeps community priorities on the agenda.

This blueprint is a statement of intent and a public commitment. It describes the priorities the candidate intends to pursue and the standard by which he asks to be judged. It is offered in the spirit of an honest conversation and should be shaped by the voices of the people it seeks to serve.

“A constituency prospers when its representation is active, accessible and accountable.”

The Ogunlade Blueprint for Ibarapa East
The case for change

Active, accessible and accountable representation

Communities want better roads to markets, stronger schools, accessible healthcare, safer surroundings and real chances for young people to earn a living. Meeting these expectations takes steady leadership, constructive engagement and work across all relevant levels of government.

Representation

Carry the concerns, needs and aspirations of Ibarapa East to the floor of the Oyo State House of Assembly and to the attention of the executive.

Lawmaking

Sponsor and support legislation that protects citizens, encourages enterprise and strengthens local institutions.

Oversight

Examine public programmes and budgets so that resources intended for residents are scrutinised and tracked.

Constituency service

Remain close to the community through regular engagement, accessible communication channels and opportunities for constituents to raise concerns.

The FOCUS framework

Forward-thinking Opportunities for Community Unity and Sustainability

The blueprint is organised around four pillars. Each item below is a proposed priority for advocacy, consultation and legislative engagement—not a claim of completed delivery.

01

Education & human capital

A community grows as far as its people are able to learn. Proposed priorities include:

  • Advocate for improved learning environments and support for public schools.
  • Promote scholarship and student-support opportunities for deserving and indigent students.
  • Encourage vocational, technical and digital-skills training alongside formal education.
  • Advocate for teacher welfare, professional training and recognition.
  • Support community learning, mentorship and reading initiatives.
02

Youth & opportunity

Youth should be partners in progress, not merely an audience at events. Proposed priorities include:

  • Connect young people with enterprise, training and empowerment programmes.
  • Encourage skills acquisition in agriculture, technology, trades and creative industries.
  • Establish a standing youth consultation platform.
  • Promote mentorship between experienced professionals and emerging talent.
  • Advocate for youth inclusion in local decision-making and public life.
03

Community development

Development should be felt in daily life. Proposed priorities include:

  • Advocate for the improvement and maintenance of rural roads and local infrastructure.
  • Support access to water, electricity and basic public services in underserved communities.
  • Promote stronger primary healthcare and community health outreach.
  • Encourage cooperation with relevant security agencies and traditional institutions.
  • Ensure towns and villages are heard in discussions about constituency development.
04

Enterprise & sustainability

A strong local economy helps farmers, traders and small businesses grow. Proposed priorities include:

  • Advocate for farmers’ access to inputs, markets and agricultural value chains.
  • Encourage SME growth through training, access to finance and links to relevant programmes.
  • Support women and youth traders and artisans.
  • Promote responsible development that considers land, the environment and future generations.
  • Pursue long-term sustainability so that progress can endure.
Standards of service

How constituents should be treated

Presence

Be visible and reachable in the communities represented.

Listening

Hold regular town halls, community meetings and stakeholder engagements.

Honesty

Speak plainly about what is achievable and avoid promises that cannot be kept.

Accountability

Report on activities and the progress of stated commitments.

Respect

Respect traditional institutions, religious leaders, associations and diverse community voices.

Unity

Serve the whole constituency, regardless of party, creed or background.

Delivery approach

Listen. Legislate. Advocate. Monitor. Report.

  1. Listen: consult communities to confirm priorities before acting.
  2. Legislate: support laws and motions that advance the constituency’s interests.
  3. Advocate: use relevant institutional and government relationships to pursue programmes and projects.
  4. Monitor: follow up on public commitments and budget provisions affecting the constituency.
  5. Report: share progress openly and invite honest feedback.
A shared task

Progress is a collective responsibility

No representative can build a constituency alone. This blueprint invites traditional rulers, community leaders, women’s groups, youth associations, farmers, traders, professionals and every concerned citizen to take part.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade offers himself with humility, discipline and a stated commitment to listen to the people, speak for the people and work for the people.

Building the Future. Empowering the People. Advancing Ibarapa East.

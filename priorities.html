Proposed public priorities

Priority ideas for better services, stronger communities, and accountable leadership.

The following priorities are presented as public policy proposals and campaign commitments for discussion, review, and public engagement.

Education access

Support better school conditions, learner welfare, and stronger opportunities for children and young people.

Healthcare outreach

Strengthen access to essential healthcare and responsive public health support for families.

Infrastructure

Improve roads, local amenities, and utilities that support community life and enterprise.

Youth employment

Promote skills, entrepreneurship, and practical pathways for jobs and enterprise growth.

Agriculture

Support farmers, cooperatives, and market access to strengthen food security and livelihoods.

Women & families

Expand support for women, families, and vulnerable groups through inclusive public planning.

Public accountability

Promote transparency, open communication, and visible constituency engagement in local governance.

Security & welfare

Address community safety, welfare support, and practical protections for households and neighbourhoods.

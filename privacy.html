Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This website is intended to provide public information and community updates in a respectful, secure, and transparent manner.

Information collected

We may collect basic information from visitors who contact the public information team or submit a message through the website contact form.

How it is used

Collected information is used only to respond to public enquiries, media requests, and community communication as appropriate.

Data protection

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and use it only in line with legitimate public communication needs and platform operations.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the official contact channels listed on the website.

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026

1. Scope and responsibility

This policy explains how information may be handled when visitors use this public information website. Before launch, the site owner should identify the legal entity or office responsible for data processing and provide a monitored privacy contact.

2. Information visitors may provide

If visitors contact the office by email or through a future website form, the information may include a name, contact details, community or organisation, enquiry category and message. Please provide only information needed to respond. Do not submit passwords, financial credentials, government identity numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

3. Purposes and lawful handling

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, route community feedback to the appropriate team, process media requests, maintain records of correspondence and protect the website from misuse. The site operator should document an appropriate lawful basis for each purpose and provide additional notices where required by applicable Nigerian data-protection law.

4. Sharing and service providers

Messages may be accessed by authorised personnel who need them to respond. Hosting, email and form providers may process technical or submission data on the operator’s behalf. Information should not be sold. Any disclosure required by law should be handled in accordance with applicable obligations.

5. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose or a lawful recordkeeping requirement. Appropriate access controls, secure transmission, limited permissions and incident-response procedures should be maintained. No website can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies, analytics and logs

Hosting services may generate technical logs such as request time, browser information and pages requested. If analytics, advertising cookies or other non-essential tracking tools are introduced, this policy should be updated to identify them and explain applicable choices. Do not claim that tracking is absent until the deployed configuration has been checked.

7. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, visitors may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or raise a concern about its handling. A verified privacy contact must be provided before publication. Identity may need to be confirmed proportionately to protect the person concerned.

8. Children and updates

This site is not designed to solicit unnecessary personal data from children. The policy may be updated when the website’s tools or practices change; the current version and review date should be displayed here.

Implementation note: This template is general information, not legal advice. Before enabling a form or analytics, have the actual data flows reviewed and replace all contact placeholders with verified details.

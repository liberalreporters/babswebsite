Profile & Experience

Meet Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

Corporate leader, accounting professional and APC candidate for Ibarapa East State Constituency in the 2027 Oyo State House of Assembly election.

Early life and education

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS, was born on 20 August 1980 in Eruwa, Oyo State. His educational journey spans accounting and financial management, alongside experience in several sectors of the Nigerian economy.

  • 1994 — Apode High School, Eruwa.
  • 2002 — National Diploma (ND) in Accounting, Federal Polytechnic, Ede, Osun State.
  • 2006 — Higher National Diploma (HND) in Accounting, The Polytechnic, Ibadan, Oyo State.
  • 2020 — B.Sc. in Accounting, Second Class Upper Division, Crescent University, Abeokuta, Ogun State.
  • 2025 — M.Sc. in Accounting, University of Lagos, Akoka.
Professional career

Finance, operations and corporate leadership

His career includes experience in education, manufacturing, civil construction, real estate and financial controls.

National Youth Service

Served during the NYSC programme from 2007 to 2008 as an Economics teacher at a senior secondary school in Kware Local Government Area, Sokoto State.

Accounting and internal audit

Held accounting and internal-audit roles at Tower Extrusions and Lufama Global Concepts Ltd.

Civil construction

Worked as an accountant with WS FZE Civil Construction Company at the LADOL Free Zone, Tarkwa Bay, Lagos.

Financial management and real estate

At Portal Realities Ltd, progressed from Internal Control to General Manager Finance and served as Deputy Managing Director / General Manager Finance from 2020 to 2024. He currently serves as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Skylark Homes and Properties Ltd.

Political and community engagement

Participation, party service and local connections

Political involvement

Ogunlade states that he has been active in progressive politics since 2003. He contested as an Assembly aspirant in 2023 and stepped down in support of the party candidate.

Party support

He reports supporting party administration in 2022 through the donation of a generator and payment of party-house rent, and hosting party members on 1 January in successive years.

Community associations

He is associated with the Intellect Club of Eruwa, Eruwa Youth Movement and Federation of Eruwa Student Union.

Student leadership

He served as President of the Federation of Oyo State Student Union at the Federal Polytechnic, Ede, in 2002.

Personal

Family and interests

Hon. Ogunlade is married with three children. His stated leisure interests include spending time with family and friends and watching football.

Public role

Candidate for Ibarapa East

He is the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for Ibarapa East State Constituency in the 2027 Oyo State House of Assembly election. His proposed priorities are set out separately in the Ogunlade Blueprint.
